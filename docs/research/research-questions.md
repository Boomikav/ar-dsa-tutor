# Research Questions & Formal Hypotheses

**Project:** A Generative AI-Driven Augmented Reality Framework for Personalized Data Structures and Algorithms Learning  
**Short Name:** AR-DSA Tutor  
**Protocol Version:** 1.0.0 (Phase 12 Hardening)  

---

## 1. Primary Research Question

* **RQ0:** Does an adaptive DSA learning framework that combines deterministic algorithmic visualization, learner modeling, generative-AI pedagogical guidance, and AR/interactive 3D visualization provide measurable improvements in DSA learning outcomes compared with a non-adaptive conventional digital learning condition?

---

## 2. Secondary Research Questions

* **RQ1 (Learning Gain):** Does the system improve DSA conceptual and procedural knowledge from pre-test to post-test?
* **RQ2 (Personalization):** Does learner-state-driven adaptive difficulty produce different task trajectories for learners with different demonstrated proficiency?
* **RQ3 (Misconception Remediation):** Does the system reduce recurring algorithmic misconceptions across repeated attempts?
* **RQ4 (Retention):** Do learners retain knowledge after a delayed post-test?
* **RQ5 (AI Guidance):** Does grounded AI tutoring provide useful pedagogical explanations without violating deterministic algorithmic correctness?
* **RQ6 (Visualization / AR):** Does interactive spatial visualization improve comprehension and engagement relative to conventional 2D presentation?

---

## 3. Formal Two-Sided Hypotheses

All primary hypotheses are formulated as formal two-sided tests without pre-judging the direction of effect:

### Hypothesis 1 — Learning Gain
* **$H_{0,1}$ (Null):** There is no statistically significant difference in normalized learning gain between the adaptive AR-DSA Tutor condition and the conventional digital learning condition.
* **$H_{1,1}$ (Alternative):** The adaptive AR-DSA Tutor condition differs significantly from the conventional digital learning condition in normalized learning gain.

### Hypothesis 2 — Misconception Reduction
* **$H_{0,2}$ (Null):** There is no significant difference in recurring misconception frequency between conditions or across repeated learning opportunities.
* **$H_{1,2}$ (Alternative):** Recurring misconception frequency differs significantly between conditions or across repeated learning opportunities.

### Hypothesis 3 — Delayed Retention
* **$H_{0,3}$ (Null):** There is no significant difference in delayed retention scores (measured at 7 days) between conditions.
* **$H_{1,3}$ (Alternative):** There is a significant difference in delayed retention scores (measured at 7 days) between conditions.

### Hypothesis 4 — Personalization & Trajectory Diversity
* **$H_{0,4}$ (Null):** Learner-state-based adaptation does not produce measurable differences in task difficulty trajectories among learners of differing demonstrated proficiency.
* **$H_{1,4}$ (Alternative):** Learner-state-based adaptation produces measurable differences in task difficulty trajectories among learners of differing demonstrated proficiency.

---

## 4. Methodological Invariant Notice
* The research questions and hypotheses must never be converted into claims of educational superiority prior to conducting actual human-subject trials under approved institutional ethics protocols.
