# Empirical Experimental Protocol

**Project:** A Generative AI-Driven Augmented Reality Framework for Personalized Data Structures and Algorithms Learning  
**Short Name:** AR-DSA Tutor  
**Protocol Version:** 1.0.0 (Phase 12 Hardening)  

---

## 1. Study Overview & Ethics Boundary

This experimental protocol defines a controlled, randomized between-subjects study comparing the full **AR-DSA Tutor** framework against an active **Conventional Digital Learning** baseline.

> **CRITICAL INSTITUTIONAL NOTICE:**
> Human-subject evaluation requires appropriate institutional review board (IRB) / ethics approval and informed consent before recruitment, data collection, or analysis of identifiable participant data. No human empirical trials have been conducted to date.

---

## 2. Experimental Conditions

```text
Condition A: Conventional Digital Learning (Active Baseline)
├── Standard text explanations and asymptotic complexity charts
├── Deterministic 2D step visualization
├── Fixed linear sequence of practice exercises
├── Static error notifications without adaptive AI hints
└── Non-adaptive fixed difficulty (No dynamic step-up or step-down)

Condition B: Adaptive AR-DSA Tutor (Full System)
├── Deterministic DSA Engine (Authoritative State)
├── Real-Time Multi-Dimensional Learner Modeling
├── Adaptive Difficulty Policy Engine (Foundational -> Challenge)
├── Multi-Agent Generative AI Pedagogical Guidance
└── Interactive 3D / WebXR Augmented Reality Visualization
```

### Causal Attribution Limitation
This two-condition experimental design evaluates the **combined intervention package** of the adaptive AR-DSA Tutor framework. It does not isolate the individual causal contribution of generative AI versus spatial AR visualization versus adaptive difficulty.

---

## 3. Future Multi-Arm Component Ablation Study

To isolate individual component causal drivers, a future 4-arm ablation study is pre-specified:

* **$C_1$ (Baseline):** Conventional Digital Learning (2D static visualizer, fixed difficulty).
* **$C_2$ (Adaptive + 3D):** Deterministic engine $+$ learner model $+$ adaptive difficulty $+$ 3D visualizer (No AI tutor).
* **$C_3$ (Adaptive + 3D + AI):** Deterministic engine $+$ learner model $+$ adaptive difficulty $+$ 3D visualizer $+$ multi-agent AI tutor.
* **$C_4$ (Full System + AR):** Full framework including WebXR AR camera passthrough and spatial anchoring.

---

## 4. Participant Eligibility & Privacy Safeguards

### Inclusion Criteria
1. Currently enrolled in or completed an introductory programming course.
2. Age 18 years or older.
3. Informed consent provided under an approved institutional protocol.

### Exclusion Criteria
1. Prior professional software engineering experience $> 3$ years.
2. Uncorrected visual impairment precluding interaction with 3D/AR displays.

### Privacy & Data Protection
* **Pseudonymous Identifiers:** Participants are assigned unique random tokens (e.g., `P001`, `P002`).
* **Zero PII Collection:** The system does not collect or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, institutional IDs, or IP addresses.
* **No Camera Storage:** The local WebXR runtime processes video frames locally; no camera imagery is stored or transmitted to servers.

---

## 5. Assessment Instruments & Timeline

1. **Pre-Test ($T_0$):** 5-item standardized parallel-form test across representative topics (Array, Binary Search, Bubble Sort, BST, BFS).
2. **Learning Intervention ($T_1$):** Complete interactive study session (Condition A or Condition B).
3. **Post-Test ($T_2$):** 5-item parallel-form isomorphic assessment (same difficulty and skill, novel concrete values).
4. **Delayed Post-Test ($T_3$):** Administered after a configurable retention window (default: 7 days).
5. **System Usability Scale (SUS):** Official 10-item Likert questionnaire administered immediately following post-test.
