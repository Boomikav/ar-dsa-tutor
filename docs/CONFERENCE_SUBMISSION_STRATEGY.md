## 1. Targeted Submission Category Decision

Based on the verified evidence from our audit:
- **Supported Track:** **Technical Prototype / Systems Tool Demonstration / Work-in-Progress (WiP)**.
- **Unsupported Track:** Full Educational Effectiveness / User Study (blocked by lack of human participant data).

## 3. Preparation Roadmap & Timeline

1. **Step 1 (Selected Track):** Target **ACM CHI Late-Breaking Work / Interactivity** or **IEEE VR Research Demo**.
2. **Step 2 (Paper Scope):** Focus on the **Three Core Validated Contributions**:
   - The unified WebXR continuous multi-algorithm architecture.
   - The sub-millisecond Execution-Grounded Explainable Adaptation (EGEA) policy.
   - The instructional discrimination demonstrated on the Independent Engineering Challenge Suite.
3. **Step 3 (Explicit Demarcation):** Explicitly present the lack of human participant evaluation as an open research agenda, inviting community replication and multi-institution classroom trials.
